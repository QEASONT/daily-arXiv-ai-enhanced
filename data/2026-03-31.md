*Sitt Min Oo,Olaf Hartig*

Main category: cs.DB

TL;DR: 通过在RDF图物化时结合用户查询，实现映射剪枝，大幅提升查询场景下的物化效率。

  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有RML映射方法不考虑用户查询，导致每次必须物化整个RDF图，效率低下，尤其在用户查询频繁变动的动态环境下问题更为突出，需提升部分物化以应对高效查询的需求。

Method: 正式定义SPARQL查询对基于RML映射生成RDF数据的可满足性，基于此对RML映射进行剪枝策略，仅物化与用户查询相关的部分知识图谱，并在GTFS-Madrid基准上进行了评估。

Result: 本文提出了一种利用SPARQL查询可满足性对RML映射进行剪枝的方法，从而仅对部分RDF图进行物化，而不是一次性物化整个知识图谱。通过这种方法，可以大幅降低RDF图的生成时间和体积，同时提升查询性能。

Conclusion: 与传统完整物化方式相比，本文方法显著减少了RDF物化时间和图规模，并提升了查询效率，适用于动态查询和联邦查询场景。

Abstract: Current approaches for knowledge graph construction with RML focus on full RDF graph materialization without considering user queries. As a result, mapping engines are inefficient in dynamic query environments, materializing large graphs even when only a small subset is needed to answer user queries. In this paper, we formally define satisfiability for SPARQL queries with respect to RDF data obtained via RML mappings and use this property to prune RML mappings for partial RDF graph materialization. Evaluation on the GTFS-Madrid benchmark shows that pruning significantly reduces materialization time, and RDF graph size while also noticeably improving querying time. Thus, enabling existing materialization engines to efficiently support generating RDF graphs in dynamic federated querying environment where user queries change frequently.

Abstract: We propose Process-Aware Policy Optimization (PAPO), a method that integrates process-level evaluation into Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) through decoupled advantage normalization, to address two limitations of existing reward designs. Outcome reward models (ORM) evaluate only final-answer correctness, treating all correct responses identically regardless of reasoning quality, and gradually lose the advantage signal as groups become uniformly correct.
